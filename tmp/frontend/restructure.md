I would restructure **Altas Farm as a real agricultural company first, and a network/member program second**, while keeping the network mechanics completely transparent.

The important caveat is that **website structure alone cannot make an MLM-related business eligible for PayMongo**. PayMongo currently places multi-level marketing in its restricted-business category, requiring prior written approval, while pyramid/get-rich-quick schemes and businesses they determine to be deceptive or financially risky are prohibited.

So the goal should not be to _hide_ the network model. The goal should be to make the site accurately communicate:

> **“Here is a real poultry business, here is what we actually sell and operate, here is how customers and partners interact with it, and here is exactly where the network compensation program fits.”**

That would also make the company look substantially more credible to a business-minded visitor.

# 24. The "business-savvy visitor" test

I would design the website so that someone experienced in business can answer these questions within five minutes:

```text
What exactly does Altas Farm sell?

Where does the product come from?

Who operates the farms?

Where is the company located?

Who are the customers?

How does Altas Farm make money?

What does a partner actually buy?

How is an order fulfilled?

What are the risks?

What happens if something goes wrong?

Who is legally responsible?

Can I contact a real person?

Can I see evidence of actual operations?
```

If the website answers those questions cleanly, it stops looking like a recruitment website and starts looking like an **agricultural business with a structured distribution/partner model**.

One final point: PayMongo's current onboarding guidance requires a public, functioning online presence with PHP pricing, Philippine address, Terms, Privacy, Refund/Return Policy, and customer contact details. ([PayMongo][3]) Your proposed redesign should therefore treat those as the **minimum compliance floor**, not the goal. The real goal is to make the underlying business model understandable and documentable enough that a compliance reviewer can determine exactly what PayMongo would be processing.
